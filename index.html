  2. Understand Pricing Cards: Recommended Bid (Base) is your internal revenue target without taxes. Official GeM Bid includes 18% GST (the exact figure to enter on GeM).

Technical Sourcing & Procurement Gaps

Manage unmapped items identified in tenders that require provisional costing, vendor quotes, or in-house engineering.

How to resolve unmapped tender requirements:
  1. Identify Gaps: When a tender asks for something not in the master catalog (e.g. 5.8GHz VTX or custom release bracket), it appears here.
  2. Add Provisional Cost: Click "+ Add Custom Component" to assign a budget price so sales can quote without waiting for long RFQs.
  3. BOM Auto-Injection: Custom items are immediately added to the active BOM and saved into the Master Catalog for future bids.

BOM Assist Workspace User Guide ×

1. Tender Ingestion & Requirement Extraction

Drag and drop any official GeM Tender PDF. The engine tokenizes the document, strips out unmapped Devanagari/Hindi font boxes, detects strict statutory clauses (such as Zero Chinese Origin, 5-Day Advance Samples, and Reverse Auction rules), and maps requirements to mandatory hardware, testing, and document checklists.

2. Compliant BOM Explosion & Cost Rollup

The system automatically builds a 100% Non-Chinese compliant BOM using verified parts from your 140-item master sheet. It calculates the raw hardware BOM, adds a 5% Warranty Reserve, 4% Contingency, and an Officer Inspection buffer, giving you both the Base Bid (Company Revenue) and the Official GeM Bid (including 18% GST).

3. Unmapped Items & Sourcing Gaps

If a tender asks for an exotic item or custom bracket not found in the current catalog, enter a provisional budget price so the quotation is never delayed. Once added, it injects into the active BOM and saves permanently to your catalog.

4. Exporting Deliverables

Click "Download CSV" for an Excel-compatible spreadsheet, or "Export PDF" for a clean, formal specification document.
